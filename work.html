    Mabble

    A little web app for Scrabble nerds (me included).

    1. Snap a photo of a finished board.
    2. Replay the game move by move: who played what, the scores, even a best guess at each player’s rack.
    3. Analyse every play with Quackle, an open-source Scrabble engine, to see the strongest moves and how far off yours were.
    4. Practise against yourself and get each move reviewed as you go.
    • Free
    • Runs in your browser
    • Bring your own word list, CSW24 welcome
