  • 01

    Mabble

    Mabble is a little web app I built for Scrabble nerds (me included). Snap a photo of a finished board and it works out how the game could have gone, move by move: who played what, the scores, and even a best guess at each player’s rack. It hooks into Quackle, an open-source Scrabble engine, so you can see which plays were the strongest and how far off yours were. There’s also a practice mode where you play against yourself and get your moves reviewed as you go. It runs entirely in the browser, it’s free, and you can use your own word list, like CSW24 if that's what you want to play with.
